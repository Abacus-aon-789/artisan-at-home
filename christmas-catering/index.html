Christmas Catering in Auckland

From intimate Christmas dinners to festive long lunches and end-of-year parties, Artisan at Home brings restaurant-quality festive catering to your table across Auckland and the greater North. Our private chefs cook everything from scratch, in your own home — so you can pour the wine, gather your people, and soak up the evening while we take care of the rest.

Whether it's a plated Christmas feast for the family, a relaxed shared table for friends, or an end-of-year celebration for the team, we build a seasonal festive menu around you — no set packages, just proper, generous food made with care.

December fills up fast, so the sooner we chat, the better.
