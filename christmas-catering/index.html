Christmas Catering in Auckland

From intimate Christmas dinners to festive long lunches and end-of-year parties, we have our seasonal Christmas menu with the firm favourites and some with a special Artisan at Home twist. We do all the preparation, cooking and clean-up so you can pour the wine, gather your people, and soak up the evening while we take care of the rest!

We have a few dates throughout December still available, so your Christmas gatherings can be less stress and more festive fun! We also have a festive menu available as a delivered option — just mention this upon enquiry.
