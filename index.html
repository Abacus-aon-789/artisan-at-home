Who we are

Hello!So glad you found us - my money's on someone recommending us, or maybe you tasted our food at an event we catered? That's how most of our business is done, our product speaks for itself, and people like to share the good in life. However you got here, welcome - we trust you'll find what you're looking for.

We don't make it complex around here - we're not big on set menus or a list of items for you to choose from. Like all great things, it starts with an idea and a conversation.

Working with the ebbs and flows of the seasons, and love getting creative with the whole ingredient, turning every last bit into something worth savouring.

Proper, restaurant-quality food, brought to your table across Auckland & Northland.

Abi & Jake
