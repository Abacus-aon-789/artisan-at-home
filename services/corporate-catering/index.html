Corporate Catering

Corporate Catering, but not as you know it…

Good food starts with good ingredients. We approach our catering with a low/no waste mindset, using the whole ingredient where possible — the tomato jam on our frittatas is made from the middles of the tomatoes most kitchens throw away, and our Big Green Bowl uses the whole broccoli, stems and all.

Less waste, more flavour.

We focus on making things artisanally at home — pestos, relishes, sauces, sauerkraut, dill pickles, you name it, we make it — even the salmon we smoke in-house. About the only thing we don't make is the bread — we leave that to the people who do it best.

It's how our food is packed with so much flavour and tastes the way it does.

Abi & Jake
