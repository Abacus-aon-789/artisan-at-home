How It Works

Booking a private chef in Auckland couldn't be easier.

01

Enquire

Share your date, guest count, and any dietary preferences through our simple enquiry form. We provide private chef services across Auckland and the greater North, from Clevedon to Mangawhai.

02

Consult

We talk about what you want, any favourite dishes or inspiration, and curate a bespoke seasonal menu tailored to your occasion and guests. Once you're happy with the menu, we request a 30% deposit to secure the date.

03

Enjoy

We arrive with fresh ingredients, cook, serve, and leave your kitchen spotless. You simply show up to your own table and enjoy the evening with your guests.
