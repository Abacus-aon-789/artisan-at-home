Wedding Catering

Your day, your way

When it comes to weddings, everything we do is completely bespoke. The menu, the format, the flow of the day — all designed around you. Some couples come to us knowing exactly what they want, others aren't sure where to start — either way, let's hear your ideas however formed or unformed, and we can make a plan together from there.

We work with all budgets and all visions, and we'll give you as much or as little guidance as you need. Our job is simply to make it delicious.

Based in Auckland, we regularly travel north for weddings across the Matakana Coast, Puhoi, Mangawhai and the wider Northland region — some of our favourite events have been up there. Wherever you're celebrating, the best first step is just a conversation. No obligation, no pressure — just a genuine chat about what's possible and whether we're the right wedding caterers for your big day.
